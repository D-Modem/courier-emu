## Original result: V.90 requested, connection not achieved

This is **not a successful V.90 qualification**. The analog 403 Courier on
vmhost CT 127 receives CRC-valid digital INFO0d/INFO1d and transmits a
CRC-valid INFO1a requesting V.90 to both 7900 and 7910. It subsequently
requests V.34. This narrows the failure to training after the V.90 request;
it does not yet identify the failing Phase 3/4 detector or comparison.

Do not confuse these calls with the separate successful I-modem V.90 tests.
The answering device identities for these latest calls were not independently
verified. Digital negotiation on the wire is evidence of digital capability,
not proof of a particular Eicon/MultiTech answering process.

Follow-up: [PCM training failure localization](analog-sip-v90-pcm-training-20261102.md)
verifies the current Eicon routes, exercises an existing A-law-capable account,
and captures a constant pre-descrambler word in the live V.90 receive path.
The subsequent [C5x delayed-call/block-repeat repair](c5x-delayed-call-repeat-v90-20261102.md)
fixes invalid constellation-table construction and recovers 143 CRC-valid MP
frames through the native receiver in replay. Later [7910 PPP tests](analog-sip-7910-ppp-20261102.md)
achieved PAP/IPCP and exact peer/internet ICMP at V.34, but V.90 still fell
back and the sustained-load test remains unqualified.

## Retained receive-path correction

`CourierDspBridge._queue_line_audio()` previously selected native raw-line
input only when the socket `line` existed. SIP has `_sip_line` instead, so it
used Python's preconversion to codec-rate words even with the ROM/native DSP.

`_clocked_line_input()` now selects the existing native ADC for either
socket or SIP line PCM, with the same decision when replaying pending input
after a DSP rebuild. Thus SIP samples remain on their 8-kHz source grid until
the actual codec sampling edge, including firmware retunes. The existing
native ADC FIR, interpolated phase table, source cushion and clocks are
unchanged. Non-ROM and no-peer conversion selection is retained.

Tests cover unchanged raw PCM through five codec-rate settings for both
sources, actual native source-FIFO priming, and legacy selection. This is a
hardware-boundary consistency correction, **not a V.90 fix demonstrated by
these calls**.

## Wire-level protocol evidence

`tools/analyze_v34_phase2.py` now decodes INFO1a's upstream/downstream
symbol-rate codes and `protocol_request`. The 38-bit body begins at ITU bit
12: **ITU 37:39 is body[25:28], little-endian**, not the last three body bits.
Selector 6 requests V.90; 0–5 request V.34; 7 is reserved. Tests cover all
these cases and two bodies from the actual capture.

Native-ADC recordings, approximate times on the adapter recording clock:

| Destination | Remote INFO0d | Remote initial INFO1d | Local V.90 request | Local V.34 request |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 7900 | 5.40958 s | 8.95457 s | 9.26058 s | 28.39818 s |
| 7910 | 8.62410 s | 12.28314 s | 12.58901 s | 28.84966 s |

All listed frames pass CRC. The local request frames have 46 timing-phase
votes. The earlier Python-conversion controls likewise request V.90, then
V.34 (7900: 9.27329 → 31.34466 s; 7910: 12.58527 → 31.11190 s).

This is not just missing V.90 enablement or an insufficient instruction
budget. The next diagnostic should capture the PCM receiver's retrain/tag-6
entry and its caller/state, correlated with Phase 3/4 audio. Do not assert
that Sd, TRN, Jd, or DIL specifically failed without that evidence.

## Media and payload limitations

Native-ADC calls have zero receive missing/late samples and zero active
adapter underruns. Both have two TX rebuffer events; maximum reported TX
packet gaps are **105.42 ms / 80.88 ms**. Native-ADC 7910 delivers ten
FCS-valid LCP Configure-Requests (CRC residue `f0b8`), not established PPP.
No post-CONNECT application payload was sent.

The container capture's only outbound gaps over 40 ms are approximately
101.69 s on 7900 and 45.06 s on 7910, later than the initial V.90 fallback.
This does not prove endpoint receipt or exclude other channel defects.
7900 also sends another V.34 INFO1a around 109.89 s; do not describe the
whole hold as retrain-free. These remain connection/training tests, not
sustained duplex payload qualification.

## A-law control blocked at SIP negotiation

A separate native-ADC call offered only `--sip-codec pcma` to 7900. It got
SIP **488 Not Acceptable Here**, no media/carrier, and firmware `BUSY`.
It completed naturally at 600M, exit 0, in 176.10 seconds. This is not a
failed V.90 training measurement.

Read-only `pjsip show endpoint` checks found:

- 6000: `(clearmode|ulaw)`
- 6501: `(clearmode|ulaw|alaw)`

Consequently an end-to-end A-law comparison is unavailable through account
6000 without a separately authorized configuration/account change. Nothing
was changed. This check does **not** establish companding conversion as the
cause of V.90 failure.
